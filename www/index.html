Where do I find my team ID?

Open fantasy.premierleague.com in your phone’s browser, sign in and tap Points. Tap the address bar: the number after /entry/ is your ID, e.g. fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/12. The official FPL app doesn’t show it.

Paste your 15 players, separated by commas or new lines. Add the club code if two players share a name, e.g. “Gabriel ARS”.

How the predicted points are calculated

Every player gets expected points for each match, using official FPL scoring:

Minutes. Recent games count most (65%), the season average 35%, multiplied by FPL’s chance of playing. Injured players are assumed to recover gradually.

Goals and assists. The player’s xG and xA per 90 (60% season, 40% last six games, so current form counts) as a share of his team’s attack, scaled to how many goals his team is expected to score against this opponent. Expected team goals come from each side’s attack and defence xG, with home advantage.

Clean sheets and goals conceded. Probabilities from how many goals the opponent is expected to score.

Saves, defensive contributions, bonus, cards. From his per-90 rates. Defensive contributions are the chance of hitting 10 CBIT (defenders) or 12 CBIRT (others).

Is it accurate? On GW5–10, predicting each week with only earlier data, it beat FPL-style points form (correlation 0.31 vs 0.24) and season points per game (0.26). Adding recent points form didn’t improve it, so it isn’t used. Single gameweeks are noisy: treat any gap under about 2 points as a coin flip.

Chips and plans. Bench Boost adds your bench’s points, Triple Captain triples your captain, and Free Hit and Wildcard squads are searched for the most points within your budget and the three-per-club rule. Hits cost 4. Saved free transfers are kept when you play a Wildcard or Free Hit. Selling prices aren’t public, so current prices are used.
