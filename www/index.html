Where do I find my team ID?

Open fantasy.premierleague.com in your phone’s browser, sign in and tap Points. The number after /entry/ in the address is your ID, e.g. fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/12. The official FPL app doesn’t show it, so use a browser (Chrome works) for this one step.

How the predictions work

Each player gets expected points per fixture under official FPL scoring. Inputs: his share of his team’s xG and xA, the opponent’s attack and defence strength (from xG), home advantage, clean-sheet and goals-conceded odds, saves, defensive contributions, bonus and cards. Minutes weight recent games more heavily and use FPL’s chance of playing.

Calibrated on this season: FPL gives about 35% more assists than xA, and xG-based odds overstate goals conceded. Over GW1–10 the predictions correlated about 0.75 with actual points per 90.

Strategies compare your best XI plus captain each week. Hits cost 4. Wildcard and Free Hit squads are searched for the most points within your budget and the 3-per-club rule. Selling prices aren’t public, so current prices are used.
